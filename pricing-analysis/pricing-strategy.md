> Generated: 2026-05-06 | For: GTA Tire Distributor, Greater Toronto Area, Ontario
> Current model: Retail = MSRP x 0.90 | Distributor = DC x 1.20 (fallback MSRP x 0.60)

## Executive Summary

The current pricing model applies a **flat discount across all brands**, leaving significant money on the table. Budget brands where GTA has no MAP restrictions could be marked up 30-40% over cost, while premium brands are capped by manufacturer policy. The recommended approach: **tiered pricing by brand category** instead of flat percentage off MSRP.

## 2. MAP-Restricted Brands: Maximum Pricing

These brands enforce Minimum Advertised Price. You **cannot** advertise below their floor price online.

| Brand | MAP Status | What You Can Do |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Michelin** | Strict MAP. ~5% above dealer cost is the max advertised price. | Sell at MAP online. In-store, you can offer private quotes to fleet/volume buyers below MAP (not advertised). Bundle with install/alignment to add margin. |
| **Bridgestone** | MAP enforced. 3-strike system. | Sell at or above MAP. Profit comes from the high MSRP base ($654 avg). |
| **Continental** | MAP via Excel spreadsheets to dealers. | Follow MAP for online. In-store flexibility on quotes. |
| **Goodyear** | MAP enforced. | Same approach -- at MAP online, flexible in-store for volume. |
| **Hankook** | MAP exists but less aggressive. | More room to discount. Watch for MAP floor on popular sizes. |
| **Yokohama** | MAP exists. | Follow MAP online. Moderate flexibility. |
| **Nexen / Nitto** | MAP exists on select SKUs. | Check per-SKU. Most have some room. |

### MAP Workaround Strategies
1. **Bundle pricing** -- "4 tires + install + alignment for $X" -- the bundle price isn't the tire price
2. **In-store only pricing** -- You can sell below MAP in person, just can't advertise it
3. **Fleet/volume quotes** -- Private quotes to businesses are exempt from MAP advertising rules
4. **Rebate stacking** -- Michelin, Bridgestone, Goodyear offer seasonal rebates ($70-100 per set). Advertise the rebate, not the low price.

## 4. Seasonal Pricing Strategy

### Winter Tire Season (September - December)

Winter tires are **urgency purchases**. Customers don't comparison-shop as much when the first snowfall hits.

| Strategy | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Early bird (Sep 1 - Oct 15)** | Standard pricing. Promote "book now, beat the rush." |
| **Peak season (Oct 15 - Dec 15)** | Raise budget brand prices 5-10%. Demand justifies it. |
| **Late season (Dec 15 - Feb)** | Return to standard. Stock clearance on slow movers. |
| **Off-season (Mar - Aug)** | Discount winter stock 10-15% to clear inventory. |

## 5. Package Deals: Tire + Wheel Combos

Package pricing hides the per-item margin and increases total transaction value.

### Package Margin Strategy

| Package | Components | Price Anchoring | Margin Approach |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Budget Package** | 4x ILINK/Mirage + 4x steel wheels + install | Advertise total: "$799 winter-ready" | 35-40% margin on tires, 20-25% on wheels |
| **Value Package** | 4x Sailun/Kumho + 4x alloy wheels + install + TPMS | Advertise total: "$1,499 complete" | 30-35% margin on tires, 20% on wheels |
| **Premium Package** | 4x Michelin/Continental + 4x premium alloy + install + alignment + TPMS | Advertise total: "$2,999+ complete" | 8-12% margin on tires, 15-20% on wheels, profit from services |

### Why Packages Work
1. Customers compare package price to competitors' tire-only price -- different comparison
2. Installation/TPMS/alignment services are 40-50% margin
3. Wheels have separate margin (alloy wheels: typically 20-30% markup)
4. Reduces price negotiation on individual components
